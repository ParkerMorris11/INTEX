Data Analyst

Turn raw data into actionable insights that drive business decisions.
High Demand Analytical +23% Job Growth

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Meet with stakeholders to define problems/KPIs
  • Pull and query data (SQL)
  • Clean and validate datasets (Excel/Python)
  • Build dashboards and visualizations (Tableau/Power BI)
  • Summarize findings and present recommendations to non-technical audiences

What Makes a Strong Candidate

  • Comfort with SQL/Excel from day one
  • Curiosity and attention to detail
  • Ability to translate technical findings into business language
  • A demonstrated project portfolio

Technical Skills & Tools

SQL Excel / Sheets Python (pandas) Tableau Power BI Statistics Data Cleaning R (optional)

A portfolio (GitHub/Kaggle projects), at least one internship, and a certificate (e.g., Google Data Analytics) are increasingly expected on top of coursework — the bar for "entry-level" has risen in the current market.

(Ranges vary this much because "data analyst" spans industries and seniority — cross-check the specific one you cite.).

Cybersecurity Analyst

Protect organizations from digital threats, breaches, and vulnerabilities.
Fastest Growing Technical +32% Job Growth

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Monitor networks for breaches and investigate incidents
  • Maintain firewalls/encryption tools
  • Check for vulnerabilities
  • Research emerging threats
  • Write reports on attempted attacks
  • Develop security standards
  • Build/test disaster recovery plans

What Makes a Strong Candidate

  • Analytical skills
  • Ability to explain risk to non-technical audiences
  • Creativity in anticipating threats
  • Extreme attention to detail
  • Strong problem-solving under pressure (may be on-call for emergencies)

Technical Skills & Tools

Networking (TCP/IP) Splunk / SIEM Linux Python / Scripting Firewalls / IDS CompTIA Security+ Vulnerability Scanning OSI Model

Entry-Level / Internship Expectations

Bachelor's degree in computer science or related field is typical, plus related work experience (often via network/systems administration first); professional certification (e.g., Security+, CISA, CISSP) is frequently preferred; some enter with a high school diploma plus industry certifications.

Salary & Growth

Median pay $129,180/yr (May 2025). Employment projected to grow 21% from 2025–2035 (much faster than average) — one of the fastest-growing IT roles — driven by rising cyberattacks and AI adoption; ~14,100 openings/year.

  • Create wireframes/prototypes

Risk Assurance / IT Auditor

Protect organizations from systemic risk by evaluating IT controls, data governance, and regulatory compliance.
Compliance-FocusedHigh Trust+6% Job Growth

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Test and evaluate IT systems/controls against industry standards
  • Verify compliance with regulations
  • Evaluate technical infrastructure (operating systems, networks, databases, ERP systems)
  • Write audit findings and recommendations
  • Sometimes mentor junior audit staff

What Makes a Strong Candidate

  • Precision and skepticism (auditors are meant to independently verify, not just accept, what they're told)
  • Strong writing skills for audit reports
  • Comfort presenting findings to management
  • Working toward or holding a CISA/CIA/CPA certification

Technical Skills & Tools

SOC 2COBITISO 27001CISA (pathway)ExcelSQLNIST CSFRisk FrameworksAccess ControlsAudit Documentation

Broad knowledge across platforms (OS, networks, databases, ERP); strong interpersonal/presentation skills since auditors report findings to leadership.

Entry-Level / Internship Expectations

Bachelor's degree in computer science, information systems, accounting, business administration, or finance; a Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) credential from ISACA is the standard certification and is often preferred even for early-career roles; some experienced-level postings ask for 4–5+ years of public accounting/IT audit experience, so entry-level candidates typically start in general internal audit or IT roles first.

Salary & Growth

Robert Half's 2026 Salary Guide lists general IT auditor salaries from $70,500–$101,000, IT auditor (technology-focused) roles from $108,500–$152,250, and senior IT auditors from $91,500–$128,750. ZipRecruiter separately estimates the entry-level IT auditor average at about $92,800/yr (as of mid-2026), with a typical range of roughly $72,000–$112,000 — a wide spread reflecting differences in what "entry-level" means by source.

ERP / Systems Consultant (Salesforce, SAP)

Help organizations maximize enterprise platforms by configuring systems, training users, and managing implementations.
Client-FacingHigh-Growth Consulting+10% Job Growth

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Gather business requirements from stakeholders (finance, ops, sales)
  • Configure and customize the ERP/CRM platform to match business processes
  • Document current-state vs. future-state workflows
  • Support system rollouts/migrations
  • Train end users
  • Troubleshoot issues post-launch

What Makes a Strong Candidate

  • Comfortable translating business needs into system configuration (not just technical skill alone)
  • Strong client-facing communication
  • A relevant certification (Salesforce Admin/Consultant tracks, or SAP module certifications) to signal platform competence early in a career

Technical Skills & Tools

SalesforceSAPOracleSQLBusiness Process MappingRequirements GatheringUser TrainingExcelJiraProject Management

Platform-specific configuration knowledge (SAP S/4HANA modules like FICO/MM/SD, or Salesforce Sales/Service Cloud and Flow Builder); business-process/requirements-gathering skills matter as much as the technical platform knowledge.

Entry-Level / Internship Expectations

A bachelor's degree helps but isn't always required, especially for Salesforce roles — Salesforce's own Trailhead platform lets people earn the entry-level Salesforce Certified Administrator credential (~$200 exam, no degree requirement) as a common on-ramp; SAP consulting roles more often expect a bachelor's degree in IT, business, or a related field.

Salary & Growth

Robert Half's 2026 guide lists general ERP consultant salaries from $70,500–$127,000. Platform-specific data: Salesforce-certified administrators average roughly $88,000–$100,000 (with entry-level/junior around $65,000–$85,000 per Salesforce Ben's 2025/26 survey), while SAP consultants average closer to $99,860 (PayScale) and SAP S/4HANA specialists can exceed $137,000 given a current shortage of qualified consultants ahead of SAP's 2027 ECC maintenance deadline.

IT Project Manager

Drive technology initiatives from kickoff to launch by aligning teams, managing risk, and keeping stakeholders informed.
Leadership-FocusedCross-Functional+7% Job Growth

Day-to-Day Responsibilities

  • Plan and lead IT projects from scope through delivery
  • Coordinate teams (developers, analysts, QA)
  • Track budgets/timelines/deliverables
  • Manage risks and communicate status to stakeholders
  • Ensure projects meet standards and cost targets

What Makes a Strong Candidate

  • Detail-oriented (tracking many moving pieces at once)
  • Strong communicator across technical and business audiences
  • Resourceful under budget/timeline pressure
  • Demonstrated leadership even in a junior capacity (e.g., leading a class or club project)

Technical Skills & Tools

MS Project / SmartsheetJiraAgile / ScrumRisk ManagementStakeholder ManagementBudgetingExcelStatus ReportingRACICommunication Planning

Project management methodologies (Agile/Scrum, Waterfall), scheduling/tracking tools, and general IT/application-development background so they can credibly manage technical teams.

Entry-Level / Internship Expectations

Bachelor's degree in an IT- or business-related field; Robert Half notes employers commonly expect five or more years of experience managing complex projects for the "IT Project Manager" title specifically — so most people enter project management from an analyst, developer, or coordinator role first rather than straight out of college. A PMP certification is valued but generally requires prior project experience to sit for.

Salary & Growth

The BLS's broader "project management specialists" category had a median of $102,320/yr (varying by year/dataset cited) and is projected to grow roughly 6% from 2024–2034 (faster than average, ~58,700–78,200 openings/year across the field). Robert Half's 2026 guide lists IT project manager salaries specifically from $103,500–$147,000.

A major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP), infrastructure-as-code tools, containerization/Kubernetes knowledge increasingly expected, networking and security fundamentals; cloud-specific certifications (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Administrator) are commonly used to signal skill level.

Entry-Level / Internship Expectations

Bachelor's degree in computer science/IT or related field is typical; because this is a newer specialization than traditional network administration, many candidates enter via a general IT/networking role plus a cloud certification rather than a "cloud engineering" degree specifically.

Salary & Growth

Multiple 2026 industry salary guides put entry-level cloud engineer pay roughly in the $82,000–$130,000 range depending on source and location (e.g., KORE1: $110,000–$130,000; VeriiPro: $90,000–$110,000; RockstarDeveloperUniversity: $82,000–$101,000), with average/mid-level pay commonly cited between $130,000–$155,000. Multiple sources cite BLS-adjacent projections of roughly 15% employment growth for cloud roles through the early 2030s — notably faster than the flat-to-declining outlook for traditional on-premises network/systems administrator roles, which BLS projects to decline about 4% from 2025–2035 as cloud and DevOps automation take over some of that work.
