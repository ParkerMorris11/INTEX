Junior Core Case · Student Prototype

About this prototype. Built for the BYU Information Systems Junior Core Case Competition. This is student work and is not an official BYU Information Systems Department tool. It is a single self-contained HTML file with no server, no database and no network calls, so it runs offline from a local file.

Where the information comes from. Career facts, salary figures and interview questions are drawn from published sources, each linked at the point of use:

  • Placement and salary for BYU graduates: BYU IS Careers placement statistics (classes of 2023–2025).
  • National pay and job outlook: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook.
  • Roles with no single BLS occupation code: Robert Half, Glassdoor, PayScale, ZipRecruiter and KORE1 salary guides, labelled industry est. throughout.
  • Interview questions: candidate-reported interviews on Glassdoor, plus hiring guides from Indeed, Tech Interview Handbook, Salesforce Ben, InterviewPrep and Infosec.

AI disclosure. AI tools were used to help research sources, draft model answers and write code. Every figure was checked against the linked source, and the feedback engine contains no AI — it is deterministic rule-based analysis that the team can explain line by line.

Salary figures change. Verify against the linked source before relying on any number here in a negotiation.
